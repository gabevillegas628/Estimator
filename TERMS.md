## 3. Estimates only — human verification is always required

**This is the most important term in this document.**

The Tool produces *unofficial estimates*. Every figure it displays or prints
is an approximation generated from the inputs and configuration values
supplied to it.

- Output of the Tool is **not** an award notice, an award letter, an offer of
  aid, a promise of funding, an enrollment agreement, or a statement of
  amounts owed.
- Output of the Tool **must be independently reviewed and verified by a
  qualified human** — a financial aid administrator or equivalent — before it
  is relied upon, communicated to a student or applicant, entered into any
  system of record, or used as a basis for any decision.
- The Tool is **not a system of record.** Official figures come from a
  school's own financial aid systems and the applicable federal, state, and
  institutional determinations, not from this Tool.
- Figures produced by the Tool **may differ** from those on any school's
  official worksheets and award documentation, including for reasons of
  rounding, proration method, and configuration values that have not been
  updated.

Estimates depend on values configured within the Tool, including tuition,
book and kit charges, program length, Pell award tables, and annual federal
loan limits. **These values become stale.** Keeping them current and correct
is the responsibility of whoever operates and uses a given instance of the
Tool, not of Wildtype.

The Tool does not verify eligibility, does not track prior aid received
elsewhere in the award year, and does not account for every circumstance that
affects an actual award.
